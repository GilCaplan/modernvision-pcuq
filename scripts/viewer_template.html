The digit chips on the 2D tab use a real trained checkpoint that turns out to be speckle-like at its one fixed noise level (see the warning in that tab's readout). This panel sidesteps that: the "denoiser" here is a closed-form, exact Bayes-optimal MMSE estimator for a world where the clean image is exactly one of two real MNIST test-set digits (index 195 = "3", index 184 = "8") — no network, no checkpoint, nothing that can be speckle-like. Drag t and σ, press Step, and the exact same math (Tweedie’s formula, Jacobian-vector products, power iteration) plays out on fully legible digit pixels.
